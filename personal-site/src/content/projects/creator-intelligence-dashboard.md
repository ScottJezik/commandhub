The Creator Intelligence Dashboard is a personal analytics product for understanding why content performs—not just how many views it receives.

It brings together post analytics, audience signals, content formats, niche patterns, and experiment planning in one place. The broader question is how a creator can use data without losing the human and creative part of the work.
