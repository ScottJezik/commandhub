Geopolitics is not separate from technology. Infrastructure, supply chains, energy systems, communications networks, and standards all distribute power.

Writing about those systems does not require treating every question as a partisan argument. It requires tracing relationships: who controls a resource, who depends on it, what incentives are created, and what happens when a system is stressed.

This is the part of geopolitics I want to explore: the material and institutional layer underneath the headlines.
