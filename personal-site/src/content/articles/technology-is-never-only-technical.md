Technical systems do not exist in a vacuum. A tool arrives inside a network of habits, incentives, institutions, and expectations.

That means a technically elegant solution can still fail if it ignores the people who have to use it, the organizations that have to support it, or the power relationships that shape who benefits from it.

The useful question is not only “Can we build this?” It is also “What kind of behavior, relationship, and world does this make easier?”
